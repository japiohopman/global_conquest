rules! RISK — Complete Rule Manual
1. Game Overview

Risk is a turn-based strategy board game of global domination. Players control armies, conquer territories, form continents for bonuses, and eliminate opponents through dice-based combat.

Players: 2–6 (best with 3–5)

Play Time: 1–3+ hours

Objective: Achieve the chosen victory condition (usually total world domination)

2. Game Components
Required Components

Game Board

World map divided into 42 territories

Territories grouped into 6 continents

Army Pieces

Infantry (1 unit)

Cavalry (5 units)

Artillery (10 units)

Risk Cards (Deck)

42 territory cards

2 wild cards

Each card shows:

A territory

A unit symbol (Infantry, Cavalry, or Artillery)

Dice

5 six-sided dice (3 attacker, 2 defender)

3. Continents & Bonuses
Continent	Territories	Bonus Armies
North America	9	+5
South America	4	+2
Europe	7	+5
Africa	6	+3
Asia	12	+7
Australia	4	+2

To receive a continent bonus, a player must control every territory in that continent.

4. Setup
Step 1: Choose Colors

Each player selects an army color and takes all matching pieces.

Step 2: Determine Initial Armies

Based on number of players:

Players	Starting Armies
2	40 each
3	35 each
4	30 each
5	25 each
6	20 each
Step 3: Claim Territories

Option A: Random Setup (Official Standard)

Shuffle territory cards

Deal all cards face down to players

Players place 1 army on each territory they receive

Option B: Manual Placement

Players take turns claiming unoccupied territories

Place 1 army per claimed territory

Step 4: Place Remaining Armies

After all territories are claimed:

Players take turns placing remaining armies on their owned territories

Step 5: Determine First Player

Roll dice; highest roll goes first

Play proceeds clockwise

5. Game Turn Structure

Each turn has three phases, in order:

Reinforcement Phase

Attack Phase

Fortification Phase

6. Reinforcement Phase

At the start of your turn, you receive new armies.

6.1 Territory Bonus

Count territories you control

Divide by 3 (round down)

Minimum reinforcement is 3 armies

Example:
14 territories → 14 ÷ 3 = 4 armies

6.2 Continent Bonus

Add bonuses for each continent you fully control.

6.3 Card Trade-In Bonus (Optional but Standard)

If you have 3 or more cards, you may (or must, if holding 5+) trade in a set.

Valid Card Sets:

3 Infantry

3 Cavalry

3 Artillery

1 of each (Infantry + Cavalry + Artillery)

Any set using Wild cards

Trade-In Values (Escalating)
Trade-In #	Armies
1st	4
2nd	6
3rd	8
4th	10
5th	12
Each after	+5

If you trade in a card matching a territory you control, you may place 2 extra armies on that territory.

7. Attack Phase

You may attack as many times as you want, or not at all.

7.1 Attack Rules

You may only attack from a territory with at least 2 armies

You may only attack adjacent enemy territories

Attacks are optional

7.2 Dice Combat System
Attacker:

May roll 1–3 dice

Must leave 1 army behind

Defender:

May roll 1–2 dice

Depends on armies in defending territory

Dice Comparison:

Dice are sorted highest to lowest

Compare highest dice pairs

Defender wins ties

Losses:

One army lost per comparison

Up to 2 losses per roll

Example:

Attacker rolls: 6, 4, 1

Defender rolls: 6, 3

Result:

Defender wins tie on 6 → attacker loses 1

Attacker wins 4 vs 3 → defender loses 1

7.3 Capturing a Territory

When the defender loses all armies:

Attacker must move at least the number of dice used in the final roll

Territory is captured

7.4 Eliminating a Player

If you conquer a player’s last territory:

You take all their Risk cards

If this puts you at 5+ cards, you must trade in immediately

7.5 Card Reward

If you conquer at least one territory during your turn:

Draw 1 Risk card at the end of your turn

8. Fortification Phase

At the end of your turn:

You may move armies once

Between two connected territories you control

Must leave at least 1 army behind

Some house rules allow unlimited fortification, but official rules allow only one move.

9. Victory Conditions
Standard Victory

World Domination: Eliminate all other players

Optional Mission Rules

Some versions include Secret Mission cards, such as:

Conquer specific continents

Eliminate a specific player

Control X territories with Y armies

10. Two-Player Rules (Optional)

For 2 players:

Use a neutral army (third color)

Neutral armies are placed but never attack

Adds balance and prevents runaway victories

11. Alliances & Diplomacy (Unofficial but Common)

Alliances are not binding

Players may negotiate, betray, or cooperate

No rule enforcement beyond social agreement

12. Common House Rules (Optional)

Fixed card trade-in values

No minimum 3-army reinforcement

Capital Risk (defend capitals)

Fog of War (hidden armies)

Time limits per turn

13. Strategy Basics (Optional Guidance)

Secure small continents early (Australia, South America)

Control choke points

Avoid overextending

Save cards for big turns

Eliminate weak players to gain cards

14. End of Game

The game ends immediately when:

One player fulfills the victory condition
