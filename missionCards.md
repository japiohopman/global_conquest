🌐 Territory Control Mission (1 card)
12. Conquer 24 territories of your choice

Each territory must be occupied by at least 1 army

Continents do not matter

📜 Important Mission Rules

Missions are secret

You may not reveal your mission unless required by house rules

The game ends immediately when a player completes their mission

Continent bonuses still apply normally

Card trading works the same as standard Risk

⚠️ Common Clarifications

You do not need to hold continents simultaneously unless the mission says so (all listed must be held at once)

If you lose a continent after completing your mission, it does not matter — victory is instant

Neutral armies (in 2-player games) do count as enemies
